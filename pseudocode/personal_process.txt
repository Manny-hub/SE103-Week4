Student Name: Ayomide Adeyeye

Problem:
I prepare my backpack every evening before a school day. I want to make sure I have everything I need so I do not forget anything in the morning.

Input:
My class timetable for tomorrow, my backpack, my laptop, and my charger.

Output:
A packed backpack with everything needed for tomorrow's classes.

Edge Case Handled:
My laptop battery is low and it cannot be charged fully before I leave.

Pseudocode:
START
  Open tomorrow's timetable
  Create an empty packing list
  FOR each class tomorrow
    Add the books and materials for that class to the packing list
  END FOR
  Put every item on the packing list into the backpack
  Put the charger into the backpack
  IF laptop battery is below 50 percent THEN
    Plug in the laptop overnight
    Place a power bank in the backpack
  ELSE
    Put the laptop in the backpack
  END IF
  Check the packing list against the backpack contents
  Zip the backpack and place it by the door
END

Why this is clear enough to follow:
Every step is a single action that can be done in order. The loop makes it clear that the same action repeats for each class. The IF/ELSE decision uses a specific number, 50 percent, so nobody has to guess what low battery means. The edge case is handled directly inside the steps. Someone who has never seen my timetable could still follow this and pack correctly.
